Questions

Questions, answered.

The short version, with the rest on the support page.

Is Left. really free?

Yes. Income, bills, expenses, the daily budget, spending limits, receipt scanning, Apple Watch, widgets, Siri, and Left Assist are all free, and Left Pro is a $9.99 one-time purchase that adds net worth, assets, and debt payoff strategies.

Is there a subscription?

No. Left. is free, and Left Pro is a one-time $9.99 purchase with future updates included.

Why doesn't it connect to my bank?

By design. You enter your own numbers, so there are no credentials to hand over and no third party in the middle, and shortcuts, receipt scanning, Siri, and Apple Watch keep logging to seconds.

Where is my data?

On your iPhone, and in your own iCloud for sync between your devices, using Apple's encryption. There are no Left. servers, no analytics, and no tracking, and the App Store privacy label reads Data Not Collected.

Does Left Assist send my questions anywhere?

No. Calculated answers come from the app on any iPhone that runs Left., open-ended questions use Apple Intelligence on iPhone 15 Pro and later, and nothing is sent to a server either way.

What do I need to run it?

An iPhone on iOS 26 or later, and watchOS 26 or later for the Apple Watch app. Open-ended Left Assist questions use Apple Intelligence, which needs an iPhone 15 Pro or later.

What happens when I get a new iPhone?

Sign in with the same Apple Account, install Left., and your data syncs back through iCloud. Pro restores automatically, and a Restore purchase button sits behind any Pro feature if you ever need it.
