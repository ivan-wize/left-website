Questions

Questions, answered.

The short version, with the rest on the support page.

Is Left. really free?

Yes. Income, bills, purchases, spending limits, trends, savings goals, receipt scanning, Left Assist, Apple Watch, widgets, Siri, and iCloud sync are all free. Left. Pro is a $9.99 one-time purchase that adds the debt payoff plan and net worth.

Is there a subscription?

No. Left. is free, and Left. Pro is a one-time $9.99 purchase with future updates included.

Why doesn’t it connect to my bank?

By design. You enter your own numbers, so there are no credentials to hand over and no third party in the middle. Logging a purchase yourself makes you notice it, and the capture bar, receipt scanning, Siri, and Apple Watch keep it to seconds.

Where is my data?

On your iPhone and Apple Watch, and in your own iCloud for sync between them. Left. has no servers of its own, no analytics, and no ads, and the App Store privacy label reads Data Not Collected.

Does it work in Airplane Mode?

Yes. Everything you need lives on your iPhone, so Left. keeps working with no connection at all. Turn on Airplane Mode and see for yourself. Changes sync through iCloud when you’re back online.

Does Left Assist send my questions anywhere?

No. Calculated answers come from the app itself on any iPhone that runs Left. Open-ended questions use Apple Intelligence on iPhone 15 Pro and later. Either way, nothing is sent to a server.

Does it work with my currency?

Yes. Pick from more than 150 currencies in Settings, or stay on Automatic to follow your Region. Amounts are never converted. Only the symbol changes, so 1,250 stays 1,250.

What do I need to run it?

An iPhone on iOS 26 or later, and watchOS 26 or later for the Apple Watch app. Open-ended Left Assist questions use Apple Intelligence, which needs an iPhone 15 Pro or later.

What happens when I get a new iPhone?

Sign in with the same Apple Account, install Left., and your data syncs back through iCloud. Pro restores automatically, and Restore Purchase is there if you ever need it.
