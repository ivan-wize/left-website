Spending

Your bills and purchases.

All in one place, and your bills carry over each month.

Logging a purchase yourself makes you notice it. Left. makes it take seconds: type what you spent into the bar at the bottom, scan the receipt, or tell Left Assist. Everyday purchases are grouped by day, bills are one tap away, and search finds any purchase in the month.

Categories

See where your money goes.

Every category, with limits where you need them.

Categories sits right in Spending and compares this month with last month as of the same day, so you’re never measuring half a month against a whole one. Set a limit where it helps, and pick categories from a grid, including Shopping, Entertainment, and Gifts & Donations.

Here: $3,636 in all, $58 less than this time in October. Food & Groceries is at $509, with $191 left of its limit.

Income

Every payday, planned.

Paychecks and side gigs, counted before they land.

Set up recurring income once and Left. lines up the paychecks you expect. When one lands, tap to confirm it, so what’s left stays true. Freelance work and other one-off income sit right alongside.

Here: two $2,273.42 paychecks and $86 from an Etsy shop received, and $350 of freelance work waiting to be confirmed.

Left Assist

Just say it.

Left Assist has its own tab. Type or talk, and it answers in your own figures.

Still private. Left Assist runs on your iPhone, and your conversations aren’t saved.

  • Talk or type

    Say it the way you’d type it. Voice is in English for now, after a one‑time download.

    • Lunch 14 and gas 40
    • Coffee 4.50

  • Add, change, or delete

    Left Assist can add, change, or delete purchases and bills. Nothing changes until you tap Update or Delete, and deletes can be undone.

  • Ask in plain words

    Answers come in your own figures, calculated right on your iPhone.

    • Am I on track this month?
    • How much is due this week?
    • How does this month compare to last month?

  • Plan, then look back

    Plans are checked against your own past months, not an outside rule. On the 1st, Month in review shows how last month went and one thing to try next.

    • Help me save $3,000 by May
    • Help me spend less on food

Left Assist is free for everyone. Some questions use Apple Intelligence, on iPhone 15 Pro and later. Calculated answers, like what’s due or what’s left, work on any iPhone that runs Left.

Questions

Questions, answered.

The short version, with the rest on the support page.

Is Left. really free?

Yes. Income, bills, purchases, categories and limits, savings goals, receipt scanning, Left Assist, Apple Watch, widgets, Siri, and iCloud sync are all free. Left. Pro is a $9.99 one-time purchase that adds the debt payoff plan and net worth.

Is there a subscription?

No. Left. is free, and Left. Pro is a one-time $9.99 purchase with future updates included.

Why doesn’t it connect to my bank?

By design. You enter your own numbers, so there are no credentials to hand over and no third party in the middle. Logging a purchase yourself makes you notice it, and the capture bar, Left Assist, receipt scanning, Siri, and Apple Watch keep it to seconds.

Do I have to add my bills every month?

No. Bills carry over to the new month the first time you open it. A bill you’ve just added is offered on the Copy card first, then carries over from the month after.

Where is my data?

On your iPhone and Apple Watch, and in your own iCloud for sync between them. Left. has no servers of its own, no analytics, and no ads, and the App Store privacy label reads Data Not Collected.

Does it work in Airplane Mode?

Yes. Everything you need lives on your iPhone, so Left. keeps working with no connection at all. Turn on Airplane Mode and see for yourself. Changes sync through iCloud when you’re back online.

Does Left Assist send my questions anywhere?

No. Calculated answers come from the app itself on any iPhone that runs Left. Open-ended questions use Apple Intelligence on iPhone 15 Pro and later. Either way, nothing is sent to a server, and your conversations aren’t saved.

Can I talk to Left Assist?

Yes. Left Assist has its own tab, and you can type or speak. Voice is in English for now and needs a one-time download. Like the rest of Left Assist, it runs on your iPhone.

Can Left Assist change my budget?

Only when you tap. Left Assist can add, change, or delete purchases and bills, and it always shows you what it’s ready to do first. Nothing is added until you tap, nothing changes until you tap Update or Delete, and deletes can be undone.

Does it work with my currency?

Yes. Pick from more than 150 currencies in Settings, or stay on Automatic to follow your Region. Amounts are never converted. Only the symbol changes, so 1,250 stays 1,250.

What do I need to run it?

An iPhone on iOS 26.1 or later, and watchOS 26 or later for the Apple Watch app. Open-ended Left Assist questions use Apple Intelligence, which needs an iPhone 15 Pro or later.

What happens when I get a new iPhone?

Sign in with the same Apple Account, install Left., and your data syncs back through iCloud. Pro restores automatically, and Restore Purchase is there if you ever need it.
