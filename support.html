Frequently asked

Is Left. really free?

Yes. The core app is completely free, with no time limits and no nagging. That includes income, bills, purchases, spending limits, trends, savings goals, receipt scanning, Left Assist, Apple Watch, widgets, and seeing what you have left to spend.

Left. Pro ($9.99 once) is for the long game: a debt payoff plan that shows what to pay first and when you’ll be debt-free, plus your assets and net worth tracked over time.

Do you have a subscription?

No. Pro is a one-time purchase of $9.99. Once you have it, you have it forever, on every device signed in with your Apple Account.

Does Left. connect to my bank?

No, and that's intentional. Left. is a manual-entry app. You log your income, bills, and purchases yourself. That keeps things private, fast, and free of surprise account linkages. Most people find that the small daily ritual is what actually changes their relationship with money.

Where is my data stored?

On your device, and optionally synced through your own iCloud account using Apple's CloudKit. Left. has no servers of its own, so it never sees your data.

If you turn off iCloud for Left. in your iPhone settings, all your data stays exclusively on your device.

Does the AI send my data to a server?

No. Smart categorization, receipt scanning, and Left Assist all run entirely on your iPhone. Your conversations with Left Assist aren’t saved, and your financial data never leaves your iPhone for AI processing. It’s never sent to OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, or anyone else.

Can Left Assist change my budget?

Only when you tap. Type “coffee 4.50”, “paid rent”, or “got paid”, and Left Assist shows you what it’s ready to do. Nothing changes until you tap, and you can undo a purchase right after.

Do I need Apple Intelligence to use Left Assist?

Not for most of it. Anything Left. can work out from your own numbers (what's due this week, how much you have left to spend, where your money went) is answered by the app itself, on any iPhone that runs Left.

Open-ended questions, the ones that need reasoning rather than arithmetic, use Apple Intelligence, which needs an iPhone 15 Pro or later. Either way the answer is computed on your device, and either way it's free.

How do I restore my Pro purchase?

Open the app, tap any locked Pro feature, and tap Restore Purchase on the paywall.

Make sure you're signed in with the same Apple Account you used to buy Pro originally.

What if I switch phones?

Your data syncs through your iCloud account. Sign in to your new phone with the same Apple Account, install Left., and your bills, purchases, income, and accounts will all be there. Pro restores automatically.

What do I need to run Left.?

An iPhone running iOS 26.1 or later. The Apple Watch app needs watchOS 26.

Left Assist's open-ended questions use Apple Intelligence, which needs an iPhone 15 Pro or later. Everything else, including the calculated answers, works on any iPhone that runs Left.

Does Left. work on Apple Watch?

Yes. See what’s left and your daily amount at a glance, log a purchase in a tap, confirm paychecks, and check off bills as you pay them, right from your wrist. Changes show up on your iPhone too.

I have a feature request.

Please send it. We keep a running list and prioritize what people actually ask for, not what we think they should want. support@left-budget.com

How do I delete my data?

Open Settings inside the app and tap Start Fresh. To remove the iCloud copy as well, go to your iPhone's Settings → [Your Name] → iCloud → Manage Account Storage → Left., and delete the data there.

Something's not working.

Sorry about that. Email support@left-budget.com and include your iPhone model, iOS version, and a quick description of what happened. A screenshot helps a lot. We'll get back to you fast.
