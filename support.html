Frequently asked

Is Left. really free?

Yes. The core app is completely free, with no time limits and no nagging. That includes tracking income, bills, transactions, and seeing what you have left to spend.

Left Pro ($9.99 once) is for people who want the long-term picture: debt payoff strategies that show which balance to clear first and your payoff date, net worth tracked over time, and savings goals.

Do you have a subscription?

No. Pro is a one-time purchase of $9.99. Once you have it, you have it forever, on every device signed in with your Apple ID.

Does Left. connect to my bank?

No, and that's intentional. Left. is a manual-entry app. You log your income, bills, and transactions yourself. That keeps things private, fast, and free of surprise account linkages. Most people find that the small daily ritual is what actually changes their relationship with money.

Where is my data stored?

On your device, and optionally synced through your own iCloud account using Apple's CloudKit. We don't have servers. We never see your data.

If you turn off iCloud for Left. in your iPhone settings, all your data stays exclusively on your device.

Does the AI send my data to a server?

No. Smart categorization, receipt scanning, and Left Assist all run entirely on your iPhone. Your financial data never leaves it for AI processing. It's never sent to OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, or anyone else.

Do I need Apple Intelligence to use Left Assist?

Not for most of it. Anything Left. can work out from your own numbers (what's due this week, how much you have left to spend, where your money went) is answered by the app itself, on any iPhone that runs Left.

Open-ended questions, the ones that need reasoning rather than arithmetic, use Apple Intelligence, which needs an iPhone 15 Pro or later. Either way the answer is computed on your device, and either way it's free.

How do I restore my Pro purchase?

Open the app, tap any locked Pro feature, and tap Restore purchase at the bottom of the paywall.

Make sure you're signed in with the same Apple ID you used to buy Pro originally.

What if I switch phones?

Your data syncs through your iCloud account. Sign in to your new phone with the same Apple ID, install Left., and your bills, transactions, income, and accounts will all be there. Pro restores automatically.

Does Left. work on Apple Watch?

Yes. Check your budget, mark bills paid, and log expenses straight from your wrist. The Watch app syncs with your iPhone through iCloud, so changes show up everywhere.

I have a feature request.

Please send it. We keep a running list and prioritize what people actually ask for, not what we think they should want. support@left-budget.com

How do I delete my data?

Open Settings inside the app and tap Start Fresh. To remove the iCloud copy as well, go to your iPhone's Settings → [Your Name] → iCloud → Manage Account Storage → Left., and delete the data there.

Something's not working.

Sorry about that. Email support@left-budget.com and include your iPhone model, iOS version, and a quick description of what happened. A screenshot helps a lot. We'll get back to you fast.
